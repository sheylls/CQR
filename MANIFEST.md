# Release Contents

This release contains the inductive CQR training and evaluation pipeline.

## Included

- the CQR model and structural reasoning modules in `cqr_mass/`;
- training, evaluation, dataset-listing, and smoke-test entry points in `scripts/`;
- configurations for FB15k-237, WN18RR, and NELL-995;
- inductive v1-v4 G1/G2 splits for all three benchmarks; and
- dependency, method, and dataset-provenance documentation.

## Excluded

Generated checkpoints, run histories, logs, caches, local diagnostics, experimental branches, and pretrained model files are not part of the public release.
